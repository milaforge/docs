# About

***

### I build software

* **An idea that needs to become real**\
  Turn a product idea into something customers can actually use.
* **A demo that needs to become a product**\
  Replace shortcuts and unfinished pieces with software that can support real users.
* **An MVP that needs to grow**\
  Make the product more reliable, maintainable, and capable of handling increasing demand.
* **An existing system that's holding the business back**\
  Find and fix the problems that make software difficult to change, unreliable, or risky.

### I secure it — and help when things go wrong

* **Before something goes wrong**\
  Identify how the system could be misused or attacked, and prepare for those scenarios.
* **When something goes wrong**\
  Investigate what happened, understand the impact, and help contain the problem.
* **After something goes wrong**\
  Help restore the system, understand the root cause, and reduce the chance of it happening again.
