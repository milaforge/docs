# Revision

Revision was a mobile gaming platform where the _backend API_ had to support large numbers of players at the same time.

As usage grew, the existing backend needed to handle much more traffic without simply adding more servers and increasing hosting costs.

I improved the backend so it could handle roughly **10× the previous throughput**, supporting **thousands of concurrent players without increasing hosting costs**.

The result was that the product could handle significantly more users without requiring a proportional increase in infrastructure spending.
