# Khavar

### Problem

A Car manufacturer wanted to provide a transparent, and verifiable mechanism for its customers,  to assemble their vehicle and follow its customization progress without having to rely entirely on information from the dealer.

### What I did

I turned the process into a working product where configuration choices and progress could be recorded and checked on-chain. I built the [MVP](../../founding/what-is-a-software-mvp/) end to end, from requirements to the working system.

### Outcome

A working MVP was delivered in about **3 months**, giving the concept a concrete product that could demonstrate how the process would work.
