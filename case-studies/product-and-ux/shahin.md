# Shahin

### **Problem**

Detecting vehicle traffic was only useful if staff could easily answer practical questions such as **"Who entered, and when?"**

### **What I did**

I built an offline system that recorded vehicle activity and added custom reporting. I worked with the people using the system to decide which information was actually useful, rather than exposing raw detection data.

### **Outcome**

Non-technical staff could get the security information they needed without depending on technical staff to interpret the data.
