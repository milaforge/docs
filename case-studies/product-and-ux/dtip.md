# dTip

### **Problem**

Sending a cryptocurrency donation normally assumes that the user already understands wallets and blockchain transactions.

### **What I did**

I designed a simple web flow for non-technical donors while keeping the donations non-custodial and publicly verifiable. The MVP connected the web interface directly to the underlying donation system.

### **Outcome**

A donor could make a transparent on-chain donation without needing to understand the underlying system in detail.
