# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community.

Everyone is expected to treat others with respect, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

* Be respectful and constructive.
* Assume good intent, but address harmful behavior directly.
* Give and accept constructive feedback.
* Keep discussions focused on ideas and contributions, not individuals.
* Respect people's privacy.
* Take responsibility for mistakes and correct them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not acceptable:

* Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
* Trolling, insults, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions or interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, enforcement may include:

1. A private warning or request to correct the behavior.
2. Temporary restrictions from participating.
3. Permanent removal from the community.

Serious or repeated violations may result in immediate removal.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainers through the repository's private contact channel or by opening a private report where available.

Reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, comments, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
