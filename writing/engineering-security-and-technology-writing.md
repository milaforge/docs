# Engineering, Security & Technology Writing

I write about things I am trying to understand, things I have built, things I disagree with, and problems I keep encountering in software.

The subjects vary, but the common thread is practical reasoning.

### Engineering

Architecture, programming, code quality, production systems, performance, and software development.

### Security

Security engineering, vulnerabilities, threat modeling, secure design, and lessons from incidents.

### Product

Building software products, technical decisions, MVPs, experiments, and the relationship between product requirements and engineering.

### Ideas & opinions

Things I think are wrong, misunderstood, overcomplicated, or worth examining more closely.

Not every article is a tutorial. Some are investigations, some are experiments, and some are simply me trying to work something out.
