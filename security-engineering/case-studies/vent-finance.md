# Vent Finance

VENT handled client digital assets. A bug in the transaction flow could therefore mean a direct financial loss.

I treated deposits, withdrawals, and other money-moving operations as attack surfaces rather than assuming that the normal user flow was enough.

I checked the boundaries between the backend and on-chain transactions, hardened the services and contracts, and worked through findings from external security audits.

### **Outcome**

about $4M in client assets were managed over three years with no major security incidents. The product also reached a 9/10 external security audit score from _Hacken Security Audit Team_.
